Ambient sound credits — TutuWorld, 2026-11-04

Ocean waves: “Beach Ocean Waves”, jasinski (submitted by qubodup).
https://opengameart.org/content/beach-ocean-waves
CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Original Freesound recording: https://freesound.org/people/jasinski/sounds/18363/
Files wave_01, wave_02 and wave_03 were rearranged, crossfaded, level-adjusted, converted to mono 32 kHz and encoded as a looping Ogg Vorbis stream.

Seagulls: “Solo Seagull Sound Effects”, compiled by Rango Mango.
https://opengameart.org/content/solo-seagull-sound-effects
CC0 1.0 as published on the source page.
Ambient calls 1–3 were level-adjusted, edge-faded and spaced across a 48-second mono Vorbis loop. No attack or death calls are used.

Creek: “Stream Sounds”, kurt.
https://opengameart.org/content/stream-sounds
CC BY 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Original file: stream-waterfall/stream1.ogg from stream-waterfall.zip.
Modified: trimmed/crossfaded for continuous looping, DC removed, level-adjusted, converted to mono 32 kHz and re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis.
